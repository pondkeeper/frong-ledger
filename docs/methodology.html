Methodology

Every number on the ledger is reconstructed from public on-chain data and can be independently verified. This page explains exactly how — and where the estimates have known limits.

Data sources

  • Transfers, holders, balances — the Robinhood Chain Blockscout indexer.
  • Historical prices — 5-minute candles for the main FRONG/WETH v4 pool from GeckoTerminal, covering the token's entire life. Candles older than 48 hours are stored at hourly resolution.
  • Live price — DexScreener, polled from your browser every 30 seconds.

Cost basis & PnL

  • Each tracked wallet's complete FRONG transfer history is fetched and kept.
  • Every inbound transfer is priced at the candle covering its timestamp; the wallet's basis is the weighted average of all inflows.
  • Every outbound transfer realizes PnL against the running average and reduces the basis proportionally (average-cost accounting, not FIFO).
  • Unrealized PnL = current balance × (live price − average entry). It updates live in your browser as the price moves.

Verification

After each refresh, the balance implied by a wallet's reconstructed history is compared to its actual on-chain balance. A mismatch first triggers a live balance re-read (to rule out a race with a trade landing mid-refresh); if the history still doesn't reconcile, the wallet's entire transfer history is re-fetched from scratch that same run. Each refresh also re-fetches the last hour of every wallet's transfers wholesale, so same-second trades can't be skipped and nothing is ever double-counted.

Whale flow

Net flow sums every tracked wallet's inbound minus outbound FRONG per hour. Transfers between two tracked wallets are tagged internal and excluded from the flow, the verdict's buyer/seller counts, the movers list, and realized PnL — internal shuffling can't fake a signal anywhere on the site.

No survivorship bias: "tracked" is not just today's top 50. A wallet that sells its way off the list stays tracked (the most recently active stay first, up to 80 total), so a whale's exit dump still counts in the flow, the verdict, and the movers list — it is labeled sold out of top 50 instead of silently disappearing.

The flywheel numbers

Harvest events are detected as FRONG inflows to the protocol fee recipient, which pairs its share and re-deposits it into the pool with the resulting LP position sent to the burn address. "FRONG recycled" is the cumulative sum of those inflows — fees that became permanent liquidity instead of being extracted. (Verified on-chain: every harvested FRONG to date has ended at the Uniswap position manager, i.e. inside the pool.) To be precise about what "locked" means: the liquidity position is locked forever; the recycled FRONG inside it is still buyable like any pool token. Only burned FRONG is gone from the supply.

Known limitations

  • Wallet-to-wallet receipts are priced at market on receipt (tagged xfer-in). If a whale moved coins between their own wallets, their true cost is older and usually lower than shown.
  • Candle pricing uses the main pool's 5-minute close. Trades executed during violent moves, or routed through smaller pools at worse prices, carry small pricing noise — fine for rankings and magnitudes, not for tax accounting.
  • Very high-frequency wallets (thousands of transfers) may be truncated and are tagged partial where so.
  • The any-wallet lookup reads up to 400 recent transfers in your browser; older history beyond that is omitted and flagged.
  • LP positions, and FRONG held inside protocol contracts, are out of scope — the ledger tracks wallet balances only.

Refresh cadence

A pipeline recomputes everything every ~10 minutes and commits the result; every commit is a permanent, public, timestamped snapshot. The page you're reading always shows the latest baseline plus a live price layer on top.
